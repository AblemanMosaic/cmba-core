# Code of conduct

Project spaces should be respectful, technically rigorous, and safe for people
to question assumptions. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate
misrepresentation, and publication of another person's private information are
not acceptable.

Discuss evidence and claims rather than attacking people. State uncertainty,
correct mistakes openly, and preserve disagreement when it has not been
resolved. Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and restrict
participation to protect these norms.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact
mechanism configured in the public repository.
